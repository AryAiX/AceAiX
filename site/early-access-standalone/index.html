What we do with this

Short version: one email, then nothing until you have an account.

  • ✓One email, on launch day. No newsletter, no drip sequence, no "just checking in".
  • ✓Never sold, never shared. Your address goes to our own database and to the tool that sends that one email. Nowhere else.
  • ✓Under 18? We never hold your address. Only a parent or guardian's, and the database refuses a row that breaks that rule.
  • ✓One click to leave. Every email carries an unsubscribe link that works immediately.
  • ✓No tracking pixels on this page. No analytics script, no advertising tag, no cookie banner — because there is nothing to consent to.
